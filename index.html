TallUp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Data storage

TallUp stores all of your data (profile, measurements, metrics, settings) locally on your device, in a local database. TallUp has no backend and never sends your data anywhere.

The only copy that can leave your phone is the one made by your own device backup — iCloud on iOS, Google One on Android — which is enabled and controlled by you, in your own account, and never accessible to us. You can exclude TallUp from it in your device settings.

No account, no tracking, no ads

TallUp requires no account and collects no personal data. The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK, no advertising, and shares no data with third parties.

Estimates

Adult-height estimates are statistical calculations performed entirely on your device, based on the information you enter. They are not a medical diagnosis and do not replace advice from a health professional.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by the Apple App Store on iOS. TallUp itself receives no payment or identity information; the store's own privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Data deletion

You can permanently erase all data at any time from Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

TallUp is intended for users aged 13 and over, and for parents tracking their children's growth. Since no data is ever transmitted to us, no personal data of any user — minor or adult — is collected or processed by us.
